# Escalation Rules

## Severity Levels

### Critical

Immediate executive escalation is required when there is:

- Severe customer impact
- Regulatory exposure
- Public reputation risk
- Service outage affecting core operations

### High

Escalation to crisis response leadership is required when there is:

- Significant service degradation
- Multiple customer complaints
- Potential compliance concern
- Delayed operational response

### Medium

Operational monitoring is required when there is:

- Limited customer impact
- No immediate compliance concern
- Manageable resource requirement

## Approval Gates

Human approval is required before:

- Sending public communications
- Notifying regulators
- Offering customer compensation
- Suspending services
- Making legal or liability-related statements
