# Crisis Management Policy

## High Severity Incidents

A high severity incident exists when one or more of the following conditions are present:

- Major service disruption
- Significant customer impact
- Compliance or regulatory exposure
- Executive escalation
- Reputational risk

## Required Response

For high severity incidents, the crisis response team must:

1. Activate the crisis response workflow.
2. Notify executive leadership.
3. Assess customer impact.
4. Review legal and compliance implications.
5. Prepare stakeholder communications.
6. Assign operational owners.
7. Record all decisions in the audit log.

## Human Approval Requirements

The following actions require human approval:

- Public announcements
- Regulatory notifications
- Customer compensation decisions
- Major service shutdown decisions
- Legal admissions
- External partner communications

## Audit Requirements

Each recommendation must include:

- Decision rationale
- Policy reference
- Risk level
- Owner
- Approval status
- Timestamp
