# Responsible AI & Safety

CrisisOps IQ is designed as a responsible enterprise AI prototype for crisis response and executive decision support.

This project follows a governance-first design and does not automatically execute sensitive operational, legal, financial, or public-facing actions.

---

## Human Oversight

CrisisOps IQ keeps humans in control.

High-impact recommendations require human approval before any action is taken.

Examples of decisions requiring human approval include:

- Public announcements
- Regulatory notifications
- Customer compensation decisions
- Major service shutdown decisions
- Legal admissions
- External partner communications
- Major resource reallocations

---

## Transparency

The system is designed to explain why a recommendation was made.

Each recommendation should include:

- Crisis context
- Reasoning summary
- Policy reference
- Risk level
- Approval requirement
- Suggested owner

---

## Auditability

CrisisOps IQ maintains an audit-ready decision record.

The audit log records:

- Recommendation
- Reasoning
- Supporting policy reference
- Risk level
- Human approval requirement
- Status
- Timestamp

This supports accountability during crisis management.

---

## Data Protection

This demo uses synthetic or redacted data only.

Do not upload:

- Confidential information
- Personal data
- Customer data
- Employee data
- Privileged legal documents
- Passwords
- API keys
- Access tokens
- `.env` files
- Production configurations

---

## Limitations

CrisisOps IQ is a hackathon prototype.

It is not a replacement for:

- Professional crisis management judgment
- Legal advice
- Compliance review
- Executive decision-making
- Regulatory reporting obligations

All sensitive decisions must remain subject to human review and approval.
