The Challenge

Enterprise Crisis Response is Critically Fragmented

When operational disruptions strike, critical information is scattered across dozens of disconnected systems. Decision-makers lack the unified intelligence needed for fast, governed response.

📋

Fragmented Reports

Incident reports arrive from multiple channels with no unified intake or classification system.

📧

Siloed Emails

Critical context buried across email threads, invisible to decision-makers who need it most.

📝

Unstructured Meeting Notes

Decisions and commitments from crisis calls never surface as structured operational records.

🗄️

Inaccessible Policies

Governance playbooks and escalation rules locked away in SharePoint, never applied in context.

⛓️

Broken Approval Chains

No traceable path from recommendation to decision. Accountability gaps at every escalation level.

👥

Scattered Stakeholders

Communications drafted ad-hoc with no governance review. Inconsistent messaging compounds the crisis.

0% of enterprise crises escalate due to delayed decision-making
0× longer average response time without unified operational intelligence
0% audit coverage with ad-hoc, manual crisis management approaches
The Solution

From Signal to Governed Action — End-to-End

CrisisOps IQ is not a chatbot. It is a Microsoft 365–native Enterprise War Room Agent that orchestrates specialized AI agents through governed, auditable workflows with mandatory human approval gates.

01

Crisis Classification

Multi-dimensional classification across incident type, operational impact, regulatory scope, and stakeholder exposure — applied consistently at scale.

02

Severity Scoring

Quantified severity assessment using weighted scoring models grounded in organizational policy and historical incident patterns from Dataverse records.

03

Root Cause Analysis

Structured extraction of causal chains from reports, meeting notes, and operational records — surfaced in executive-ready format.

04

24-Hour Action Planning

Time-boxed response plans with resource allocation, task assignments, and escalation paths — generated and ready for executive review.

05

Human Approval Gates

No autonomous high-risk execution. Every critical recommendation passes through Power Automate–governed approval workflows with traceable decision records.

06

Stakeholder Communications

Governance-reviewed communications for executives, internal teams, customers, and regulators — drafted by the Communications Agent, approved before dispatch.

07

Audit Logging

Every agent action, decision, and approval is recorded in the SharePoint Audit Repository — creating immutable, compliance-ready operational records.

08

Executive Reporting

Board-level crisis summaries, operational impact assessments, and response effectiveness reports — delivered directly to Microsoft Teams and Outlook.

Multi-Agent System

Six Specialist Agents — One Governed Response

Each agent is purpose-built for a distinct crisis response function. Orchestrated by CrisisOps IQ. Governed by human approval at every critical decision point.

📥
Intake Agent
Input Processing

Extracts and normalizes facts from reports, emails, meeting notes, and documents. Converts unstructured crisis signals into structured incident records ready for analysis.

→ Output
Structured incident facts with source attribution
🎯
Crisis Classifier Agent
Severity Assessment

Determines incident severity, escalation level, and business impact using policy-grounded scoring models. Classifies against organizational risk thresholds and regulatory requirements.

→ Output
Severity score (0–100) and priority escalation level
🗺️
Operations Planner Agent
Response Planning

Generates time-bounded response plans with resource allocation, task assignments, and escalation paths. Recommendations are explainable and policy-aligned before human review.

→ Output
24-hour action plan with assigned resources
⚖️
Compliance Agent
Governance Review

Identifies applicable governance requirements, regulatory obligations, and approval prerequisites. Flags compliance gaps and routes incidents through appropriate approval chains.

→ Output
Compliance review findings and approval routing
📢
Communications Agent
Stakeholder Comms

Drafts governance-reviewed communications for executive leadership, internal teams, customers, and external stakeholders — tailored to audience, context, and regulatory requirements.

→ Output
Approval-ready multi-audience communications
🔒
Safety & Verifier Agent
Responsible AI

Validates all agent recommendations against source evidence. Prevents unsupported conclusions, flags confidence gaps, and enforces responsible AI principles before governance approval.

→ Output
Verified, explainable, governed response package

Governance & Safety

Human-in-the-Loop. Always.

CrisisOps IQ enforces responsible AI principles through architectural design, not policy promises. Every critical action requires human authorization.

🧑‍⚖️

Human Approval Gates

All high-impact recommendations require explicit human authorization through Power Automate approval workflows before any action proceeds.

⚖️

Compliance Review

Dedicated Compliance Agent reviews every response plan against regulatory obligations, with mandatory sign-off before execution.

🚫

No Autonomous Execution

CrisisOps IQ never executes high-risk actions autonomously. The separation between recommendations and completed actions is architecturally enforced.

🔐

Synthetic Data Only

Configured and validated using synthetic or redacted data exclusively. No real personal, privileged, or confidential data enters the demo environment.

📋

Complete Audit Trail

Every agent action, human decision, and workflow event is captured in the SharePoint Audit Repository — creating immutable, compliance-ready records.

🧠

Explainable Recommendations

All agent outputs include source attribution and confidence signals. Recommendations are traceable to specific policies, records, and evidence — never black-box outputs.

⚠️
Responsible AI & Data Notice

This demo uses synthetic or redacted data only. Do not upload confidential, personal, privileged, or sensitive information. CrisisOps IQ is designed for enterprise deployment with proper data governance, access controls, and compliance reviews in place.
